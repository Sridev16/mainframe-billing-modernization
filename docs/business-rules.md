# Billing Business Rules
1. Active customers are billable.
2. Inactive customers are rejected.
3. Unknown customers are rejected.
4. Up to 500 units: $0.50/unit.
5. 501–1000 units: $0.45/unit.
6. Above 1000 units: $0.40/unit.
7. Usage above 1000 gets 10% volume discount.
8. PREMIUM customers get an additional 5% discount.
9. Tax uses customer-specific tax rate.
10. Minimum taxable charge is $25.
11. Previous balance is added to the final bill.
12. Invoice number is billing month + customer ID.
